# Lichess forum copy

## The rule this needs to respect (verified 2026-08-15, primary source)

Source: https://lichess.org/page/forum-etiquette

> **Advertising and spam.** Posts that are purely promotional are not welcome in the forum. We ask
> that you keep all advertisements outside of Lichess. This includes asking others to join your
> team or tournament, or check out your YouTube channel... Don't make threads for: "follow me",
> "join my team", "join my simul", "subscribe plz". Don't reply to the thread, giving it more
> attention.

And on sub-forum choice:

> On Lichess, there are four sub-forums... Share your own game in Game Analysis and report bugs in
> Lichess Feedback.

This is a strict rule and it is enforced by deletion, not a warning first. **Recommendation: do not
post a dedicated "I built X, check it out" launch thread on the Lichess forum at all.** The rule
text's own example of what NOT to do ("check out your YouTube channel") is structurally identical
to "check out my app," even though this app is free and non-commercial - the forum doesn't
distinguish by motive, it distinguishes by whether the post's primary purpose is advertising.

The one path that is clearly compliant: **post in the Game Analysis sub-forum, sharing one real,
specific game** - which is explicitly what that sub-forum is for - and mention the tool only as
supporting context for how the analysis was produced, not as the headline. This mirrors the
r/chessbeginners approach in reddit-chessbeginners.md: contribute the genuine thing the community
values (an interesting analysed game) and let the tool be a footnote, not a pitch.

## What NOT to do

- Do not title a thread "I built a free chess trainer, check it out" - this is the exact pattern
  the etiquette page calls out.
- Do not cross-post the same announcement to multiple sub-forums.
- Do not ask anyone in the thread to "check it out," "try it," or "give feedback on the app" as the
  primary call to action - keep the call to action about the chess discussion.
- Do not repeat this post. One well-chosen real game, once, is the ceiling for this channel per the
  campaign plan (CAMPAIGN.md treats Lichess forum as a very low-reach, high-goodwill channel, not a
  growth channel).
